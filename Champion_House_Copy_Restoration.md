### Roof Week

**Heading:** The Roof Comes First

**Body copy:**

> Restoration began where it was needed most urgently: the roof. Water damage doesn't wait, so before any cosmetic work could start, both the house and the barn needed new roofs to protect everything underneath them.
>
> Over the weekend of August 1–2, 2026, a crew tore the old barn roof down to bare decking, framed in new plywood sheathing, and laid fresh asphalt shingles — while the house's own gambrel roof was stripped to bare wood shingles near the dormers and chimney. It was loud, dusty, and genuinely satisfying work to watch.

**Images:** `Screenshot 2025-04-23 at 2.28.37 PM.png` (earlier aerial, tarped/patched sections mid-repair) as an early marker of the problem, followed by the `IMG_8584.jpeg`–`IMG_8614.jpeg` sequence (six photos: barn tear-off with crew, cupola, and dumpsters staged on the lawn; new sheathing and shingles going onto the barn; the house's gambrel roof stripped near the dormers and chimney, bunting still hanging on the facade below)
